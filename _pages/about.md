**About Me**

Hello! I’m Avash Palikhe, a Ph.D. student at the Knight Foundation School of Computing & Information Sciences (KFSCIS) at Florida International University, where I conduct research under the supervision of Dr. Fahad Saeed. I am a Graduate Research Assistant at the Precision Computational Health and Biomedical Data Science Lab (PCDS Lab), also known as the Saeed Lab. My research focuses on Algorithmic Fairness, Health AI, and Trustworthy AI. I have also delivered tutorials at IJCAI’25, CIKM’25, and ICDM’25.

Thank you for visiting my website! Feel free to explore my work or  [reach out](mailto:avashpalikhework@gmail.com) if you'd like to connect or collaborate.
